Harmonic Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Emotive Studios LLC handles information in connection with the game Harmonic Drive.

1. Information We Collect

Harmonic Drive does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personally identifiable information.

The game may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as settings, progress, scores, calibration preferences, locally imported songs, locally imported chart files, and other save data required for normal gameplay.

2. Local Audio and Chart Files

Harmonic Drive may allow users to import local audio files and chart files for personal gameplay use. These files remain on your device unless you choose to move, copy, delete, or share them outside of the game.

Harmonic Drive does not upload your local audio files or chart files to Emotive Studios LLC.

3. Purchases

Purchases are processed by the platform where you obtained the game, such as Google Play. Harmonic Drive does not directly collect or store payment card numbers, billing addresses, or other payment credentials.

Platform providers may process information necessary to complete purchases, subscriptions, refunds, entitlement checks, and fraud prevention according to their own privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Platform Services

Harmonic Drive may interact with platform services depending on the version of the game you use. These may include Google Play services.

These services may collect limited technical, device, account, purchase, crash, diagnostic, or gameplay-related information according to their own privacy policies and platform settings. Emotive Studios LLC does not control how these third-party platform providers process data.

5. Analytics and Advertising

Harmonic Drive does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell user data.

If analytics, crash reporting, or similar diagnostic services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what data is collected and how it is used.

6. How Information Is Used

Information stored locally by the game is used to provide gameplay functionality, save progress, remember settings, support calibration, load local songs and charts, and improve the player experience.

Platform-provided information may be used to process purchases, verify entitlements, provide platform features, prevent fraud, and maintain game functionality.

7. Sharing of Information

Emotive Studios LLC does not sell personal information.

We may rely on platform providers such as Google to provide purchases, distribution, platform features, and account-related services. Any information processed by those providers is handled according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Harmonic Drive stores local gameplay data on your device for as long as the game remains installed or until you delete the data.

You can delete local game data by using in-game options where available, deleting imported files, clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the game.

Purchase records and platform account information are managed by the platform provider where the purchase was made.

9. Security

Harmonic Drive uses reasonable measures to protect game-related data and application integrity. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Harmonic Drive does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
